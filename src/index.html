A note on the data

This catalog uses the supplied CoStar snapshot from November 12, 2026, filtered to floor plans with a positive asking rent, reported availability and a linked Matterport scan. Per-bed pricing is excluded.

Rent ranges are asking prices for the floor plan, not quotes for a specific unit. A budget filter matches the lowest asking rent; some units may cost more. Fees are incomplete, and unknown fees are not treated as zero.

Pet and parking policies come from community records. A scan may depict a representative unit. No accessibility, structural-condition or code-compliance assessment is implied.
